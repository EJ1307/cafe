01Our story

su·se·gad

/soo-seh-gaad/ adj. Konkani

  1. Content, unhurried, at ease.
  2. The Goan art of the slow afternoon: a long lunch, a short nap, a chair on the balcão.
  3. How you’ll feel after the second cup.

See also balcão, siesta, “five more minutes”.

An old house on 31st January Road

The house was built in 1924 by a ship’s clerk who wanted blue shutters and got them. Over the next century it was a printing press, a tailor’s and, for a long while, nothing much at all.

In 2016 Anika Fernandes, who grew up two lanes away, and Joel Mathias, who had spent ten years roasting coffee in Bengaluru, took the lease on the ground floor. They scraped back four coats of paint to find the original ochre, rehung the shutters and left the balcão exactly where it was.

The old pantry became the roastery. The kitchen became a bakery, because in Goa a morning without poee is a morning that hasn’t started. The rest of the time, we mostly try not to hurry anyone.

— Anika & Joel

04Bakery & roastery

From the oven at 6 am

Everything on the counter started somewhere earlier and further away. Here is the long way round to your cup and your bun.

  1. 01Twice a year, after harvest

    Source

    Two family estates near Chikmagalur, 1,100–1,400 m up, coffee growing under pepper vines and silver oak. We drive up after every harvest, taste everything and buy what we’d drink ourselves.

  2. 02Tuesdays & Fridays, 7 am

    Roast

    Joel roasts in the old pantry on a five-kilo drum roaster, in batches small enough to taste every one. Espresso goes a shade darker; filter stays light enough to tell you where it grew.

  3. 03Every morning, 4:30 am

    Bake

    The ovens go on in the dark: poee and croissants first, then buns and bolinhas. The first tray is out by six. Bebinca, all seven layers of it, happens on Thursdays.

  4. 04Tue–Sun, from 8 am

    Brew

    Espresso on the lever machine, kaapi decoction dripped overnight in steel, cold brew steeped for twelve hours. Then somebody opens the shutters and puts the cat outside.
